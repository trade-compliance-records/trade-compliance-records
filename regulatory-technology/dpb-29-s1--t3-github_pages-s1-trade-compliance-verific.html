Understanding Trade Compliance Requirements

Trade compliance encompasses a wide range of regulations that vary from country to country. Understanding these requirements is crucial for companies engaged in global trade. Essential components include:

  • Customs compliance: Adhering to laws regarding tariffs, import duties, and classification.
  • Export controls: Complying with restrictions on goods that may pose national security risks.
  • Sanctions: Observing international sanctions against specific countries or entities to prevent unlawful transactions.
  • Record-keeping: Maintaining accurate records of all transactions for regulatory audits and verifications.
